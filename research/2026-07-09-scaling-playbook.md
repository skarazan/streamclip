# Scaling playbook — automated streamer clip channel (research, 2026-07-09)

## Headline risks/findings
- **#1 threat = YouTube "reused content" demonetization**, NOT DMCA. Zero-commentary automated clip pipelines are the top demonetization cluster in 2026. Mitigate: recurring branding/title-card identity, meme-caption overlays beyond transcript, "top X" framing on comps, consistent editorial voice.
- CaseOh/Jynxzi tolerate clip channels (community norm = free promo). Low DMCA risk, but informal — no legal protection.
- **Shorts RPM = $0.03–0.08/1k views = 3–14% of long-form.** 100k long-form views ≈ 10M Shorts views in revenue. Shorts = subscriber/discovery funnel; 14-min comps = actual revenue.
- Monetization bars: 1k subs + 4000 watch-hours (12mo) OR 1k subs + 10M Shorts views (90d).

## Format rules (all zero-edit-compatible)
- Clip length 15–30s; sub-20s = highest completion (42%).
- Hook < 2s: cut INTO the reaction, never build up. +19–30% retention.
- Word-level animated captions w/ bolded emotional keywords = standard (we have this).
- Reaction moments > gameplay-skill moments (2025 data, structural).
- Titles: streamer name + reaction verb + specific trigger; curiosity gap. Google indexes Shorts — searchable phrasing > hashtags. 2-3 tags max.
- **Loop trims**: since Mar 2025 replays count as views. End frame ≈ start frame, or hard-cut right after punchline (no dead-air tail). Pure trim-point logic.
- Keep original stream audio (no music = no extra copyright surface).
- Single moment per Short; multi-clip only in long-form comps.

## Cadence
- 1-2 Shorts/day, FIXED time slot (consistency > optimal windows). 5+/wk grows 3-5x faster than weekly.
- Windows if choosing: 7-9am, 12-1pm, 8-10pm viewer-local; weekday 12-3pm, weekend 9am-12pm.
- Community tab: 3-5 posts/wk = +15-20% impressions, no sub threshold in 2026.
- Cross-post native (re-export, NO watermark) to TikTok + IG Reels = 3-4x reach, no dup penalty.

## Top-10 action list (agent's ranking)
1. Add original framing/branding to every upload (de-risk reused-content) — DO FIRST
2. 1-2 Shorts/day fixed slot
3. Loop-optimized trim points
4. Cross-post native TikTok + Reels
5. Bias selection to reaction/emotional moments
6. Hook < 2s always
7. Disproportionate polish on comp thumbnails/titles (that's where $ is)
8. Push long-form cadence post-monetization
9. Community tab 3-5x/wk
10. Google-searchable title phrasing
